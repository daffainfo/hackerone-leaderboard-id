How this is built

HackerOne publishes no all-time per-country leaderboard, so this one is reconstructed. ALL_TIME_REPUTATION is capped at the global top 100 and ignores the country filter, and HIGHEST_REPUTATION_BY_COUNTRY requires a year. But every leaderboard entry embeds the hacker's lifetime reputation and worldwide rank — so the work is finding every Indonesian hacker, not ranking them. A daily job sweeps every dated country board from 2020 onward, annually and per quarter, then dedupes and sorts by lifetime points.

Hackers with no reputation are excluded, as are accounts that no longer exist. A hacker who earned points only before 2020 and has not placed on a country board since cannot be discovered — those boards return nothing for earlier years. A blank world rank means the hacker sits below HackerOne's global ranking cutoff.
